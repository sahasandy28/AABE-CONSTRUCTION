ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Upcoming government & industrial developments in and around Kayar – Mambakkam.

Green City is a residential plot layout from AABE DEVELOPERS at Kayar – Mambakkam. Plots are offered at a published price of ₹4,000 per sq.ft.

Kayar – Mambakkam sits on the Vandalur–Kelambakkam growth belt — the vital connector between GST Road and OMR. Kilambakkam bus terminus traffic, feeder-road upgrades, and employment centres at SIPCOT Siruseri and the Maraimalai Nagar–Oragadam industrial belt are transforming this stretch into a high-growth corridor.

With improved connectivity, nearby jobs, and expanding public infrastructure, Kayar – Mambakkam is emerging as a smart destination for land investment.

Located at the meeting point of these corridors, Green City stands as a future-ready layout for investors looking for long-term appreciation. This is the right time to invest — the region shows strong growth potential over the next two years.
