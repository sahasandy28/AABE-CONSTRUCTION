ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Upcoming government & SIPCOT developments in and around Uthiramerur.

Chola City is a residential plot layout from AABE CONSTRUCTION at Uthiramerur Village, Uthiramerur Town Panchayat & Taluk, Kancheepuram District. Starting plot size is 1,200 sq.ft. Starting price is ₹24 Lakhs. Approximate price is ₹2,000 per sq.ft.

SIPCOT industrial parks, large-scale road and infrastructure projects, and continuous government investment are transforming Uthiramerur into a high-growth corridor.

With improved connectivity, employment opportunities, water resource development, and public infrastructure, Uthiramerur is emerging as a smart destination for land investment.

Located at the centre of these developments, Chola City stands as a future-ready layout for investors looking for long-term appreciation. This is the right time to invest — the region shows strong growth potential over the next two years.
