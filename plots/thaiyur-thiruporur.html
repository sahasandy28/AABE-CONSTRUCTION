ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Upcoming government & SIPCOT developments in and around Thaiyur – Thiruporur.

Abi City is a residential plot layout from AABE DEVELOPERS at Thaiyur-B Village, Thiruporur Taluk, Chengalpattu District. Plots are offered at a published price of ₹3,990 per sq.ft.

SIPCOT Siruseri IT Park, the Thiruporur six-lane bypass, OMR widening, and OMR–ECR links are transforming Thaiyur – Thiruporur into a high-growth corridor between Kelambakkam and Thiruporur.

With improved connectivity, IT and industrial employment, and expanding public infrastructure, Thaiyur – Thiruporur is emerging as a smart destination for land investment.

Located at the centre of this OMR stretch, Abi City stands as a future-ready layout for investors looking for long-term appreciation. This is the right time to invest — the region shows strong growth potential over the next two years.

"Abi City — Thaiyur – Thiruporur. Invest today. Grow tomorrow."
